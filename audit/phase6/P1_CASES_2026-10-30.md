# Phase 6 P1 case strengthening — 2026-10-30

The seeded planner property now checks G2 foreign tunnel/session/device
preservation after each reload and shutdown, and G4 retention of a
manager-raised session's UP state when its identity is unchanged.
Foreign objects retain their identifiers, records and administrative
state. This supplements the targeted conflict tests; it does not replace
their collision cases.

F5's survivor-name case now executes both synchronous and asynchronous
deletion. The asynchronous case first observes the occupied name and
retained survivor index, advances the fake kernel, then requires
publication and later acceptance of a manager rename. F7's service
convergence case now gates both direct session creation and staged
publication; a reload accepted during convergence must be ignored in
either path.

Validation used kernel `6.8.0-147-generic`, native stable Cargo and all
features. The exact commands and suite counts are in
[all-features-current.log](entry/all-features-current.log) and the
[ten TEST_SEED repetition logs](determinism/), seeds 1 through 10.
All ordinary suites passed each repetition. Ignored live suites were
not executed by those commands. [Clippy](entry/clippy-current.log)
also passed with warnings denied.

This closes these case gaps only. P1's remaining rule-map work and the
global Phase 6 gates are still open; no strict CI flag is enabled.
